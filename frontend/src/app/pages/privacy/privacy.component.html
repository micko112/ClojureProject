Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use BeBetter, we collect:

  • Account information — username, email (optional), phone number (optional), and password.
  • Profile information — display name, bio, profile picture, and website you choose to provide.
  • Activity data — activities you log, including type, duration, intensity, and timestamps.
  • Social data — posts, comments, likes, reactions, and follow relationships.
  • Uploaded media — images and videos you upload to posts or your profile.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the BeBetter service.
  • To display your activities, posts, and profile to other users.
  • To compute leaderboards, badges, streaks, and XP.
  • To send you notifications about social interactions (likes, comments, follows).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your activity data, posts, and profile are visible to other registered users of the platform.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely. Passwords are hashed using BCrypt and never stored in plaintext. Sessions expire after 24 hours. We implement rate limiting and security headers to protect against common attacks.

5. Your Rights

  • Access — you can view all your data through your profile and activity history.
  • Correction — you can edit your profile information at any time.
  • Deletion — you can delete your account and all associated data from Settings.

6. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie is HTTP-only and expires after 24 hours. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the platform.
